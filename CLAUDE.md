# Instructions for AI assistants working in sgtuff-platform

This repo holds SGTUFF's web apps, including the new www.sgtuff.org site in `apps/web`. It sits under the parent project **sgtuff-cooperative** (the legal entity's repo).

## Brand: take it from the parent

- The brand source of truth is the `brand/` folder in [kesonlim/sgtuff-cooperative](https://github.com/kesonlim/sgtuff-cooperative/tree/main/brand) (v2, locked 23 Oct 2026). A public copy is in [kesonlim/sgtuff-brand-guide](https://github.com/kesonlim/sgtuff-brand-guide).
- Before any design or front-end work, read `brand/BRAND.md` there. If you cannot reach that repo, ask for it to be attached rather than guessing.
- Use the master logo SVGs from `brand/logo/`. Never recreate the wordmark with live text.
- Use the brand tokens (`brand/tokens/tokens.css` or `brand/tailwind.preset.js`). Key values: TUFF Red `#E8352A`, Red Ink `#C1271D`, Ink `#14161A`, Paper `#FAF7F2`, Marker `#FFD43B`, Pandan `#0E7C56`, Stone `#6B665E`. Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque (display), Inter (text), JetBrains Mono (data).
- Copy these files into the app when needed; do not edit brand files here. Brand changes are made in sgtuff-cooperative first.

## Facts to get right

- Terence Yow is Chairman and holds the Fair Tenancy Industry Committee appointment.
- SGTUFF is a registered co-operative society, not a statutory body. Do not claim official ministry partnerships.
- Member count: use only this baseline, verbatim: "Representing 700+ frontline business owners, 600+ brands and 2,600+ outlets across Singapore (as of Oct 2026)." Always show the "as of" date. Do not use the older 1,000+ figure.
